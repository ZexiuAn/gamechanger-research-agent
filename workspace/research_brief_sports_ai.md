# Sports Analytics & Team Collaboration AI Research Briefing

## Executive Summary
This briefing synthesizes current AI capabilities across sports performance analytics and team operations platforms (benchmark: Hudl, MaxPreps, GameChanger, SportsEngine).

## Key Findings
- **Video & Computer Vision**: Automated event tagging, player tracking, and instant clip generation (Hudl Focus/IQ benchmarks).
- **Mobile Stat Capture & Scoring**: Real-time pitch tracking, spray charts, and live scorekeeping (GameChanger core strength).
- **Automated Recaps & Natural Language Digest**: Post-game automated news generation and highlight packaging for parents and scouts.
- **League Operations & Scheduling**: AI-assisted tournament scheduling, roster eligibility verification, and communication bots (SportsEngine / TeamSnap).

## Competitor Comparison Matrix
| Platform | Primary Strength | AI Integration Level | Target User Base |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hudl** | Video breakdown & tactical analysis | High (CV + Auto-tagging) | High school / Collegiate / Pro |
| **GameChanger** | Mobile scorekeeping & live streaming | High (Auto recap + ML stat tracking) | Youth baseball/softball/basketball |
| **MaxPreps** | National rankings & stat aggregation | Medium (Data aggregation + editorial) | High school sports community |
| **SportsEngine** | Registration & league administration | Emerging (Workflow automation) | League operators & tournament directors |

## Risks & Items to Verify
- Verification of cross-sport model generalization accuracy.
- Edge inference latency during live streaming conditions.
